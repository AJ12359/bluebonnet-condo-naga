Our data practices comply with Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012), enforced by the National Privacy Commission (NPC). Legal bases for processing:

  • Legitimate interest — site analytics to improve user experience

You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting us on Facebook. This does not affect processing already completed.
