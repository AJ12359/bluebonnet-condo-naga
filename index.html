How to book

Booking is done securely through Airbnb — here's how it works.

1
Check availability

Click "Book on Airbnb" and pick your dates. You'll see the exact price including all fees before you confirm.

2
Send a booking request

Fill in your guest details on Airbnb and send the request. The host will confirm within an hour.

3
Pay securely

Payment is processed by Airbnb — credit card, debit card, GCash, and more. Your money is protected by AirCover.

4
Get your check-in details

After booking, the host will send the smartlock code and full check-in instructions via Airbnb message.

5
Arrive any time

Self check-in — no need to wait for the host. Just enter the code, drop your bags, and enjoy your stay.

6
Check out & review

Check out by 11:00 AM. After your stay, leave an honest review — it helps future guests and means a lot to us.
